Samosa Chaat

Ingredients

  • 2 to 3 crisp vegetable samosas, lightly crushed
  • 1.5 cups cooked white chickpeas, warm
  • 1 small red onion, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup whisked plain yogurt
  • 3 to 4 tbsp tamarind chutney and 2 to 3 tbsp green chutney
  • 1/2 cup nylon sev
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder, 1/2 tsp chaat masala, and 1/4 tsp red chili powder
  • Fresh chopped coriander leaves

Instructions

  1. Warm the cooked chickpeas with a pinch of salt and chaat masala until hot.
  2. Spread the warm chickpeas onto a serving plate and top with lightly crushed samosas.
  3. Spoon whisked yogurt over the samosas, then drizzle tamarind chutney and green chutney.
  4. Sprinkle chopped onion, sev, roasted cumin powder, remaining chaat masala, and chili powder.
  5. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve immediately while crisp.
