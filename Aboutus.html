About us

Our Story

From Our Family Kitchen to Your Table

 Spice Garden began as a small family kitchen in Mumbai, where grandmother Lakshmi's recipes were passed down through generations.
 Today, we honor that heritage while embracing modern culinary techniques to create dishes that are both familiar and extraordinary.

 Every spice is hand-selected, every sauce simmered for hours, and every plate presented with the care you'd expect at a five-star dining experience. 
 Great food brings people together — and that's exactly what we've been doing since 2008.
 
 We believe that true flavor comes from respecting time-honored cooking methods—roasting and grinding our own whole spices daily and sourcing only
 the freshest local produce. At Spice Garden, we don't just serve meals; we create memorable dining experiences that celebrate culture, community, 
 and the timeless joy of sharing great food together.
    

Milestones

  • 2010 — Opened our very first cozy 30-seat Spice Garden kitchen and dining room.
  • 2015 — Awarded "Best Authentic Regional Restaurant" by the City Culinary Guild.
  • 2019 — Launched our signature house-blended spice line and expanded to a second flagship location.
  • 2024 — Celebrated serving over 500,000 happy guests and introduced a farm-to-table seasonal menu.
